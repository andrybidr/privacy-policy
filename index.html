This Privacy Policy explains how Patterns (“we”, “us”, or “our”), the developer of Flipero (the “Game”), handles personal information when you play the Game, view its advertisements, contact us, or visit this policy page. Patterns is responsible for the processing described here, including acting as the data controller where applicable.

Flipero stores game progress on your device and uses third-party services for gameplay analytics, advertising, and marketing measurement. Device identifiers and activity data can be personal information even when they do not include your name. You can contact us to ask about your data or request deletion.

1. Information we handle

Game progress and preferences

The Game saves information such as your chosen hero name, progress, levels, quests, virtual resources, equipment, skills, tutorial progress, language, and sound settings locally on your device. These local saves are separate from the gameplay events sent to analytics services. Flipero does not require you to create a Patterns account or sign in with Facebook.

Device, usage, and advertising information

The third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the Game may automatically collect or receive:

  • Technical information: device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, language, network information, IP address, and approximate location inferred from IP address, such as country or region.
  • Identifiers: app-instance or installation identifiers, SDK-generated identifiers, and device or advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA where available and permitted by your settings and applicable requirements.
  • Gameplay and diagnostics: sessions, timestamps, tutorial completion, level and quest progress, virtual currency sources and spending, item and skill interactions, and technical error or performance information.
  • Advertising and attribution: install and referral information, campaign information, ad requests, impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completion, ad placement and network, and estimated advertising revenue.

The exact information depends on your platform, SDK configuration, device settings, and available permissions. We do not request access to your contacts, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location for the Game’s core functionality.

Information you provide to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or device details you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Why we use information

  • Operate the Game, save progress, remember preferences, and deliver in-game rewards.
  • Understand gameplay, balance levels and progression, improve features, and diagnose problems.
  • Serve and measure advertisements, including optional rewarded ads, and measure advertising revenue.
  • Understand which campaigns bring players to Flipero and evaluate and improve those campaigns.
  • Detect fraud and abuse, secure the Game, respond to support and privacy requests, and comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, the legal basis depends on the purpose: providing requested game functionality; our legitimate interests in maintaining, securing, and supporting the Game where those interests are not overridden by your rights; compliance with legal obligations; or consent for analytics, access to device information, advertising personalization, or tracking where required. Consent, where required, must be obtained separately; reading this policy or using the Game does not by itself constitute consent.

3. Third-party services and sharing

Flipero integrates the following services. Their SDKs can transmit information directly from your device to the provider. Providers may act as our processors or as independent controllers for particular activities, as described in their notices.

  • GameAnalytics: gameplay and session analytics, progression, virtual resource events, technical diagnostics, and advertising performance. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Notice.
  • AppsFlyer: install attribution, campaign measurement, selected in-game events such as tutorial completion and level achievement, advertising revenue measurement, and fraud prevention. See AppsFlyer: Processing Customer Data.
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads: ad mediation, ad delivery, rewarded ads, measurement, and fraud prevention. Advertising partners may process identifiers, technical information, IP-based approximate location, and ad interactions, and may use them for personalized advertising where permitted. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, including its advertising partner information.
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram advertising): when the Meta Ads partner integration is enabled in AppsFlyer, attribution and event information may be shared with Meta to measure and optimize Flipero advertising campaigns. This can include installation and referral information, permitted identifiers, tutorial completion, level achievement, and ad revenue events, depending on the active configuration. This integration does not require Facebook Login or the Facebook SDK in the Game and does not give us access to your Facebook profile, friends, or messages. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information to providers that help us handle support or host services, where needed to comply with law or protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger or transfer of the Game, subject to applicable protections.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising-related disclosures of identifiers and activity may qualify as “selling”, “sharing”, or “targeted advertising” under some privacy laws. Where applicable, you can request an opt-out using the contact details and controls below.

4. Your privacy choices and rights

You can manage advertising identifiers and personalization through your device’s privacy or advertising settings. On supported Android versions, you may reset or delete your advertising ID; on iOS, you can manage app tracking permissions in Settings. Available controls vary by operating system. You may also use privacy controls shown within ads, where offered by the ad provider. These controls may limit personalization without removing all ads or all processing needed for security and measurement.

You can contact hello@patterns.games to withdraw consent, object to processing, or request an opt-out from applicable advertising-related sale or sharing. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to obtain information about processing, access or receive a copy of your data, correct it, request deletion, restrict processing, request portability, or appeal a decision about your request. You may complain to your local data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

5. How to request data deletion

Email hello@patterns.games with the subject “Flipero data deletion request”. No Patterns account is required.

  1. Tell us that your request concerns Flipero and whether you use Android or iOS.
  2. Describe what you want deleted. If available and you are comfortable providing it, include an app or SDK identifier relevant to your installation. If you cannot find one, contact us first and we will help identify what is needed. Do not post identifiers publicly.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information to verify the request and locate the relevant records. We will delete or anonymize personal information we control, subject to applicable exceptions, and instruct our processors to assist where relevant. For information a provider controls independently, we will explain how to contact that provider.

We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any permitted extension or reason we cannot fulfill part of a request. Limited records may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or resolving disputes.

To remove local game progress, use your device’s app storage controls to clear the Game’s data, or uninstall the Game. Device or cloud backups may retain copies under your platform’s settings. Uninstalling does not automatically delete data already received by analytics or advertising providers; use the email process above for that information. Removing local progress may be irreversible.

6. Retention and security

Local saves remain on your device until removed through app storage controls or uninstallation, subject to platform backup behavior. We retain support correspondence and other information we control only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, operate and secure the Game, meet legal requirements, or resolve disputes. The relevant purpose, sensitivity of the data, provider settings, and legal requirements determine the retention period.

Analytics and advertising providers apply retention periods according to their notices and our applicable configuration and agreements. De-identified aggregate statistics that no longer identify you may be kept for longer. Contact us for information about records associated with your installation.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.
